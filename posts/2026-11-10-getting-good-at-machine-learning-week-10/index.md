Oh my god it's been 10 weeks since I started getting interested in machine learning. I still haven't gotten even remotely close to getting good at machine learning. I've just been getting confused by matrices and calculus for the past 10 weeks. Man learning new things is hard... I hope I get good at machine learning eventually ;-; 

I finished reading chapter 5 of the [MML book](https://mml-book.github.io/) and completed all the end of chapter exercises. However, I feel like the book explained a lot of concepts unclearly and I didn't really understand alot of the matrix calculus in the book, so I decided to find another course online that covered that material. I ended up finding this course called [Matrix Calculus for Machine Learning and Beyond](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-s096-matrix-calculus-for-machine-learning-and-beyond-january-iap-2023/) and have been working through it for most of this week. I have finished around 75% of the course and plan to finish the rest of the lectures and the problem sets by next week. I really like this course. It has helped me understand how to take derivatives of matrices more clearly and how automatic differentiation works. When I am done this course I will probably move onto learning statistics and probability.

I was thinking about how I should go about learning statistics and if there were any more productive ways to learn math than sitting in my room and just reading information/doing practice problems alone in my room until I eventually understand whatever concept I am trying to figure out. This takes multiple hours sometimes and feels really unproductive. While thinking about this, I remembered the fact that I am currently a student studying at [UBC](https://www.ubc.ca/), which means I have access to free math tutors and can attend office hours hosted by teaching assistants and professors who teach statistics classes.

I think when I am learning statistics, I am going to try and use as many UBC office hours and free tutoring sessions for understanding and clarifying math concepts as I can, as I think getting someone to teach me how to do something is more time efficient and easier than self learning. I have no idea if this is a better study strategy though as I have never really learned anything like this before, so this is kind of me experimenting with a different way of learning things. I will write back here about how effective this learning strategy is sometime in a month probably.

That's all the updates I got this week. See you next week.
